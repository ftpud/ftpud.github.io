Screenlater

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how the Screenlater iPhone app (“Screenlater”, “the app”), developed by Dmitrijs Juskovs (“I”, “me”), handles your information. In short: by default your pictures and screen content are processed on your iPhone. Content leaves your device only when you choose a cloud translation option, and then only to translate it.

What Screenlater does not do

  • No email sign-in or customer profiles. Managed translation uses a purchase-derived identifier to verify access and allowance.
  • No advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and no tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for advertising.
  • No persistent translation history is stored in Screenlater’s billing database. Content is processed in memory and may be held briefly for delivery or stream recovery, as explained below. Cloud providers have their own retention policies.

Translation options and where your content goes

You choose the translation engine in Settings. New installations start with on-device text recognition and translation. Screenlater never switches to a cloud option on its own.

Requests to OpenAI are made with response storage turned off. Cloudflare and OpenAI operate under their own privacy and data-retention policies (Cloudflare, OpenAI), which may include limited retention for security and abuse monitoring.

Live screen translation

Live translation uses iOS screen broadcasting, which you start and confirm yourself. Captured frames are processed in memory to recognize and translate text. Recent screens and their translations are cached in memory during a session so unchanged screens are not translated again; this cache is discarded when the broadcast ends. A small preview image used by the floating window is kept temporarily in the app’s private storage on your device.

Data stored on your iPhone

  • Your settings: languages, translation engine, capture mode and update options.
  • Your OpenAI API key, if you add one, stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only.
  • A local estimate of your OpenAI spending (amounts only, no content).
  • Your purchase status, cached securely so the app and its extensions can check access.

This data stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app (Keychain items may persist until you remove them through iOS).

Purchases

Purchases, the free trial and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. I do not receive your name, Apple Account, payment details or contact information. When you request managed allowance verification or use managed translation with a subscription or the free trial, the app sends Apple’s signed transaction record to the Screenlater server. The server verifies it with Apple to apply your allowance. The trial allowance is granted once from the original verified trial purchase; restoring or reinstalling does not restart it. Starting the free trial never automatically purchases a paid subscription.

Data held by the Screenlater server (managed service)

The managed service stores records when it verifies a trial or subscription, processes allowance requests or receives related App Store notifications. On-device translation does not send your translation content to this service. Service records include:

  • A purchase-derived account identifier, original App Store transaction identifier, verified trial or paid-period identifiers and dates, and entitlement status. Trial and subscription allowances are kept separately.
  • Request identifiers, request-content hashes, provider response or stream-run identifiers, usage amounts (tokens and cost), and reservations, used to enforce your allowance and avoid charging twice. Request-content hashes do not contain readable translation content.
  • Identifiers of App Store notifications already processed.
  • A hashed IP address used to limit session requests. Rows older than approximately ten minutes are removed when another session request reaches the limiter; there is no background deletion timer, so rows may remain longer during inactivity.
  • Content-free error logs (error codes and technical status, never your text, images, keys, purchase records or IP address), kept by Cloudflare for a few days.
  • Usage metrics (amounts, token counts, latency, operation type, allowance state and error codes) labelled with a short hash of the billing period, kept for about three months. They do not contain translation content or raw transaction identifiers. These metrics are pseudonymous, not anonymous: the period hash can be matched to a transaction period held in the billing ledger.

The current service has no automatic deletion schedule for financial and idempotency records, including allowance periods, request accounting and processed-notification identifiers. These records are currently retained indefinitely to preserve balances, prevent repeat grants and resolve billing issues. No shorter automatic deletion period is currently implemented.

Legal basis and your rights

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, processing for translation is based on your request to provide the service, and billing records on the performance of the contract and legal obligations. You can ask for access to, or deletion of, data associated with your managed trial or subscription by emailing me. Because no name or email is stored in the ledger, include the product and approximate purchase date to start the request. Those details alone do not establish ownership; further verification may be needed. Do not email API keys, signed purchase records or private keys. There is currently no automatic deletion flow. Any records that must be retained for an applicable legal obligation or to prevent duplicate credits will be explained when handling your request. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

Children

Screenlater is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
