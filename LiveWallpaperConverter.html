Privacy Policy for Screen Live

Screen Live (formerly Live Wallpaper Converter) turns your videos into Live Photo wallpapers. This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: editing happens on your iPhone, the developer runs no servers and collects no personal information, and anything you share is stored in your own iCloud account.

Videos and Photos

Videos you open are processed only on your device and are never uploaded by the app. You choose each video yourself through the system photo picker or the Files app.

The app asks for permission to add photos to your library so it can save the Live Photos you create. It does not read or browse your photo library.

Saved Live Photos contain no location or device information from the original video. The only metadata is the identifier that links the photo to its video.

Galleries (optional)

You can add wallpapers you made to a gallery and share it with a link. Galleries use Apple's iCloud (CloudKit):

  • Your galleries are stored in your own iCloud account, in a container that only Screen Live can access. The app cannot see your iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive or any other iCloud data.
  • A gallery contains only the wallpapers you add to it (the photo, its short video and a small preview image) and the gallery name you choose.
  • When you share a gallery, anyone who has its link can view and save its wallpapers. Links are read-only: nobody else can change or delete your gallery. If you post a link publicly, anyone can open it.
  • You can stop sharing a gallery (the link stops working immediately) or delete it, together with its wallpapers, at any time in the app.
  • Sharing is provided by Apple. Apple's iCloud share page may show the name of the iCloud account that shared the gallery. The app itself only shows the gallery name.
  • Galleries you open from a link are remembered in your iCloud account and on your device, so you can find them again. You can unfollow them at any time.

The developer does not have access to your iCloud account or to galleries you have not shared. iCloud storage is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reports and Content Moderation

If you report a shared gallery or wallpaper, the app stores a report in the app's iCloud database containing: an anonymous iCloud identifier of the gallery and its owner, the reported wallpaper (if any), the reason you selected and the date. Reports do not include your name, email address or the wallpapers themselves, and only the developer can read them. They are used only to review the reported content.

Galleries that break the rules (for example illegal, abusive or infringing content) can be blocked for everyone. You can also block a person in the app so that you no longer see their galleries.

Advertisements

Screen Live is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional ads you can watch to save a wallpaper from someone else's gallery. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads. Where required, the app asks for your consent first, and on iOS it asks for permission before ads can use your device's advertising identifier. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics

The app does not use any analytics or tracking services of its own.

Children's Privacy

Screen Live is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Content and Takedown Requests

To report content, use the Report button in the app. For copyright or other takedown requests, or if you need anything removed, email dmitrijs.juskovs@gmail.com with the gallery link and a short description. Requests are reviewed promptly.
